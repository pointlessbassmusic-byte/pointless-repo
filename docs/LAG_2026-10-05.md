# Kalshi vs Polymarket in-play lead-lag (MLB) — result (2026-10-05)

Pre-registration: `docs/LAG_PREREG_2026-10-05.md`. Code: `research/lag/`.

Data: 284 September MLB games matched across venues. Both venues settled all 284
the same way, so the matching is right. Second-stamped trades on both venues.

## Conclusion
- **No exploitable lag in either direction** at a reaction time of 2–30 s.
- A jump on one venue that the other has not followed is as likely to be noise
  (it partly reverses) as news.
- Sub-second lead-lag may still exist, but this data cannot resolve it, and it
  would mean a co-located speed race against professional market makers.
