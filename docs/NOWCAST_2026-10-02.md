## Why it fails (diagnostic, not part of the pre-registered test)

| Checkpoint (LST) | Brackets | Brier, model | Brier, market mid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11:00 | 4,709 | 0.207 | 0.131 |
| 13:00 | 3,592 | 0.218 | 0.130 |
| 15:00 | 2,369 | 0.206 | 0.098 |
| 17:00 | 343 | 0.188 | **0.044** |

- **Early in the day:** a forecast-free model only knows climatological warming.
  The market has the forecast.
- **Late in the day:** hourly METARs miss the true peak between reports, so the
  model stays split between H = M and H = M + 1, and Kalshi brackets are 2°F wide.
  The market's Brier of 0.044 at 17:00 shows traders are already watching the
  same continuous, high-resolution feed the settlement is built from.
- An edge here would need that 1–5-minute feed *and* to beat the people already
  using it to the quote. That is a latency race against incumbents, not a modelling
  edge.

## Side fix found while building this
`sportsbot/substrate_bridge/climatology.py` and `sportsbot/signals/nws.py` mapped
Chicago to O'Hare and Austin to Camp Mabry. Kalshi's rules name CLIMDW (Midway) and
CLIAUS (Bergstrom). Both are fixed, with a regression test.

## Note on settlement source
Daily-high series switched from the NWS CLI report to "The Weather Company"
(keyed by the same CLI station) between Aug 1 and Oct 1 2026. Any weather model
should now be validated against Kalshi's settled values, not CLI.
