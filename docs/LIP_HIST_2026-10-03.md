- **Where the reward matters:** in most categories, the share needed to break
  even (s* = 0.23–0.70) is 3–100× the share a 100-lot actually gets in today's
  books (0.003–0.09). The pools are real but crowded. In Sports, where a single
  pool is the largest, a 100-lot gets 0.3% of the score.
- **Where fills made money:** Mentions, Weather and Entertainment are positive
  only because the fills themselves profited, not because of rewards. That is a
  directional effect, not reward capture, and the per-day variance is large.
