Data: 3,000 sampled paid-out programs (Sep 1 – Oct 2 2026). 2,178 were simulated;
689 were skipped because their market had not settled yet, and 133 were never
quotable. Continuous 100-lot quotes on both sides at the best bid; fills from

- **Where the reward matters:** in most categories, the share needed to break
  even (s* = 0.23–0.70) is 3–100× the share a 100-lot actually gets in today's
  books (0.003–0.09). The pools are real but crowded. In Sports, where a single
  pool is the largest, a 100-lot gets 0.3% of the score.
- **Where fills made money:** Mentions, Weather and Entertainment are positive
  only because the fills themselves profited, not because of rewards. That is a
  directional effect, not reward capture, and the per-day variance is large.

## Mentions confirmation (pre-registered, Jun–Aug 2026, disjoint data): FAIL

393 programs over 68 days, same simulation code (`research/lip_hist/analyze_mentions.py`).

| Subset | Programs | Fill P&L, through | t | day-t | Fill P&L, touch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All Mentions | 393 | −$1,533 | −0.16 | −0.45 | +$41,817 |

| KXTRUMP* only | 82 | +$3,922 | 0.77 | 1.16 | +$44,323 |

- **Through fills, the ones certain to execute:** profit disappears out of sample.
- **Touch fills:** what remains positive is the Trump series, and only under the
  touch rule. That counts every trade at our price as filling us, which in
  practice requires being first in the queue.
- **Conclusion:** the September result was noise plus queue optimism. Not
  pursued further.

## Overall conclusion on Kalshi LIP
Rewards are paid, but they are spread over deep, competitive books. A 100-lot's
share is far below the break-even share in every category, and the fills on
their own do not make money once the queue is respected. The forward recorder
on the server (`deploy/sportsbot-lip.service`) is still the definitive check;
these results say not to expect it to pass.
