## Evidence on edges (and our own tests)
- **Makers beat takers on average:**
  - Kalshi: Bürgi/Deng/Whelan 2026 −9.6% vs −31.5%; Bartlett 2026.
  - These are averages over all resting orders. Our fill-level tests show that a
    slow (1-minute) maker loses to adverse selection in every category
    (SURVEY_2026-10-01, LIP_HIST_2026-10-03, CRYPTO15_2026-10-05).
- **Cross-venue gaps:**
  - In-play NBA arbitrage on Polymarket lasts a median 3.6 s, at about 15 shares
    (arXiv 2605.00864).
  - Our MLB test finds Kalshi leads Polymarket by about 3 s, with no exploitable lag
    at 2–30 s (LAG_2026-10-05).
- **Sportsbooks vs Kalshi:** pricing is now at parity on straight bets (Rotowire
  2026).
- **Speed:**
  - Official data feeds beat TV by 7–14 s (Northwestern 2018).
  - Exchanges answer this with delays for takers, and Crypto.com exempts market
    makers.
  - No exchange rule found that bans faster *public* data; MNPI and insider
    trading are banned.
- **Combos:** fair at executed prices (COMBO_2026-10-05).
