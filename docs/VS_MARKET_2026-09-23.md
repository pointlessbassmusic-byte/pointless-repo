# Do the models beat the line? No.

_2026-09-23_

Every strategy in this repo is now measured against the price it would
have to beat. None is profitable. This file records the numbers and the
method, because the previous validation compared models to *baselines*,
and a model can beat a coin comfortably while losing money on every bet.

## Method

For each settled Kalshi market: take the venue price at several lead
times before the event, score market and model against the realised
result, and settle a flat stake at the model's claimed edge, fees
charged. `sportsbot/backtest/vs_market.py` is the reusable harness.

Out-of-sample discipline:

- **MLB** — Elo ratings stop at 2026-09-06 (`last_played` per team
  confirms it). Scored only on events after that: 229 games.
- **Tennis** — the Kalshi bootstrap is fitted on settled markets, i.e. on
  the very markets under test. Refitted on matches before 2026-09-08
  only (1,412 of 1,582), then scored on events after: 271 markets.

## MLB: the model is worse than the line, and its disagreement is backwards

| lead | n | mkt Brier | model Brier | mkt LL | mdl LL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24h | 227 | **0.2343** | 0.2367 | **0.6604** | 0.6662 |
| 12h | 229 | **0.2325** | 0.2370 | **0.6567** | 0.6668 |
| 6h | 229 | **0.2307** | 0.2370 | **0.6528** | 0.6668 |
| 3h | 229 | **0.2304** | 0.2370 | **0.6517** | 0.6668 |

The line sharpens as the game nears (0.2343 → 0.2304); the model is flat,
having no new information. It beats a coin (0.2500) and always-home
(0.2469) by a hair — which is all the earlier "log loss 0.6809 vs 0.689"
result ever showed.

Flat stake at the 3h line, one contract per qualifying side:

| min edge | bets | win % | ROI/contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.03 | 130 | 37.7% | −3.10% |
| 0.05 | 84 | 33.3% | −5.16% |
| 0.08 | 39 | **23.1%** | **−10.99%** |

Losses grow with the claimed edge. The model's disagreement with the
market is not noise, it is **anti-predictive**: where the model is most
confident the market is wrong, the model is most wrong. The likely cause
is the starting pitcher — the largest single per-game factor in baseball,
absent from the model on these markets and priced by the line.

CLV is zero: correlation between model disagreement at 24h and the line
move to 3h is **+0.039**, and at material disagreement the line moves
toward the model 50.4% of the time. A coin.

Market calibration is clean in every bucket with sample (0.4–0.5: +0.012,
0.5–0.6: −0.024, 0.6–0.7: +0.050). No favourite-longshot bias to exploit.

## Two traps this harness exists to stop

**In-play contamination.** Kalshi's `occurrence_datetime` is not reliably
first pitch. Sampling at lead 0 gives market Brier **0.0631** and **91%**
accuracy — a price that has seen the result. A backtest taking "the price
at the market's start time" as the closing line would read as an
unbeatable market, or, flipped, as a brilliant strategy. `suspect_in_play`
flags any lead whose Brier collapses against the longest lead; only 24h–3h
are usable here.

**Fitting on the test set.** Ratings bootstrapped from settled markets are
fitted on the markets being scored. The harness takes an explicit cutoff
and drops earlier events rather than quietly inflating the result.

## Maker fees are real and were not modelled

`GET /series/<ticker>` reports `fee_type: "quadratic_with_maker_fees"` on
both KXATPMATCH and KXMLBGAME (verified live). Resting orders are not
free. The client implemented only `kalshi_taker_fee`, while
`bot/strategy.py` prefers maker execution via `post_inside_spread` — so
the bot priced its own preferred execution path at zero.

`kalshi_maker_fee_per_share()` now models it. The per-contract rate is
**not verified**: kalshi.com returned 429 on every fee-schedule URL when
this was written, and guessing a rate the venue will actually charge is
worse than making the gap explicit. Set
`KALSHI_MAKER_FEE_PER_CONTRACT` from the live schedule before trusting any
maker-side PnL. The default is non-zero so an unset environment
over-costs rather than inventing a free lunch.

The maker fee is **flat**, not quadratic like the taker fee. At p=0.05 it
is 75% of the taker fee (0.0025 vs 0.0033) — near the wings, posting is
barely cheaper than crossing, which is the opposite of the intuition
maker-first quoting rests on.

The same call confirmed `fee_multiplier` 0.5 for KXMLBGAME and 1 for
KXATPMATCH, matching `FEE_MULTIPLIERS`.

## What is left

Nothing directional. Every model here is worse than the price, by
0.005–0.048 Brier, on out-of-sample samples of 229 and 271.

The only untested hypothesis is **spread capture** — structural rather
than predictive, so it does not require beating anyone's forecast. It is
not obviously alive either: mean |line move| 24h→3h is 2.2¢ against
spreads of 1–4¢, so adverse selection is the same order as the spread
being captured, and maker fees come off the top. Answering it needs a live
order-book capture measuring post-fill drift, not another model.

Until something clears this harness, the correct number of dollars at risk
is zero, and the bot placing no bets is the system working.
