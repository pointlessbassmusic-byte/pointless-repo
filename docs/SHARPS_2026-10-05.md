# Follow the sharps on Polymarket sports — result (2026-10-05)

Pre-registration: `docs/SHARPS_PREREG_2026-10-05.md` (+ amendment 1).
Code: `research/sharps/`.

## Verdict: FAIL as a copy strategy. The skill is real, but it is a speed edge

**Selection (August only):**
- 1,500 candidate wallets were analysed.
- 397 were excluded as market makers.
- 915 had ≥ 20 markets.
- **52 qualified as sharps** (per-market P&L t ≥ 2), against about 21 expected by
  chance.

**Test (September, disjoint), moneylines ≥ $10k volume, Polymarket sports fee
0.05·p(1−p):**

| | n | Mean / share | day-t | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Copy at max(their price, mid +60s) + 0.5¢ (PRIMARY)** | 3,548 | **−2.18¢** | −1.97 | −3.3% → FAIL |
| Sharps at their own prices | 3,548 | **+2.75¢** | **+3.20** | **+4.4%** |
| Placebo (random eligible wallets) at their own prices | 2,351 | −0.54¢ | −0.48 | −1.0% |
| Placebo copy +60s | 2,351 | −2.14¢ | −2.46 | −4.0% |

**Skill persists out of sample.** August's sharps beat the market in September at
their own prices (t = 3.2), and random wallets do not. Copying them a minute later
does not work.

## Why (exploratory, not part of the pre-registered test)
- **When they trade:** 85% of their first buys are **in-play**: 3,006 in-play at
  +3.3¢, 437 in the 6 h before the game at −0.9¢, 105 earlier at +1.3¢.
- **How they trade:** they buy into a live price move. They pay 3.0¢ more than the
  mid 1 min earlier, and the mid moves a further +3.4¢ their way within 60 s.
- **How fast the edge decays:** a sample of 700 signals, using the next
  third-party fill of the same outcome as the copy price:

| Copy fill delay | n | Mean / share | t |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 s | 227 | +1.70¢ | 0.60 |
| 5–15 s | 181 | +0.24¢ | 0.08 |
| 15–30 s | 179 | −1.38¢ | −0.43 |
| 30–60 s | 265 | −0.21¢ | −0.08 |
| 60–120 s | 368 | −1.45¢ | −0.67 |

- **Mechanism:** the edge is reacting faster to live game events. It is priced
  within roughly 5 seconds. Copying would need sub-5-second detection and
  execution on Polymarket's main exchange, which is geoblocked for US orders and
  never circumvented here.
- **Kalshi route:** a cross-venue version (Polymarket sharp → Kalshi same game)
  would only work if Kalshi in-play prices lag Polymarket by more than a few
  seconds. That is untested and would need a forward, second-level recorder.

## Disclosed limits
- Candidate pool sampled from 800 large August markets.
- 134 heavy wallets hit the activity page cap, so their August P&L covers late
  August only.
- Minute-level price history for the primary rule.
- Trade-based copy prices for the decay table are fills by other traders, not
  quotes.
