## What it means
- **DMI/ADX:** loses about the spread plus fee in both directions. Following and
  fading the trend are equally empty, which is the inversion identity from
  `docs/STRATEGY_LAB_2026-09-29.md` again. Trend strength over 14 one-minute bars
  does not predict the 15-minute close beyond what the quote already prices.
- **Spot fair value:**
  - The normal-distribution fair value disagrees with the quote by more than
    3¢ + fee on ~93% of markets, and those disagreements lose even with zero
    latency.
  - So the market prices the Up/Down outcome better than a vol-scaled normal
    from Coinbase 1-minute bars. Likely reasons: BRTI is a multi-exchange index;
    settlement uses 60-second averages; returns have fat tails; and the order
    book carries information from faster feeds.
- **Two-sided maker:**
  - With 1-minute re-quoting, a 100-lot loses about $31 per market to adverse
    selection.
  - The cited wallets are profitable at the same activity because they re-quote in
    milliseconds and pull orders the moment spot moves; that speed is their edge.
  - Reproducing it means a low-latency spot feed plus co-located quoting, against
    established bots. On Polymarket, US order placement is geoblocked anyway.
