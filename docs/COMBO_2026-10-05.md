# Kalshi combo (parlay) maker economics — result (2026-10-05)

Pre-registration: `docs/COMBO_PREREG_2026-10-05.md`. Code: `research/combo/`.

Data: 44 two-minute windows (Aug 20 – Oct 2), 6,297 sampled combo trades with
settlement (3 dropped). Combo flow is large: about 88k combo trades and $3.1M of
YES notional in a sampled 30 minutes; takers buy YES 82% of the time.

## Verdict: FAIL

| Maker fee | Discovery (Aug 20 – Sep 10) | Confirmation (Sep 11 – Oct 2) |
|---|---|---|
| **0.035·p(1−p) (primary)** | n=3,300, **−0.74¢**/contract, day-t −0.84, −0.9% on risk | n=2,997, **+1.11¢**, day-t +2.04, +1.4% |
| 0.0175 | −0.58¢, −0.67 | +1.25¢, +2.32 |
| no maker fee | −0.43¢, −0.49 | +1.40¢, +2.59 |

- **Why it fails:** the confirmation half alone would pass, but the pre-registered
  bar also requires a positive discovery half, and it is negative.
- **Pooled:** about zero.
- **Interpretation:** at executed prices, combos are close to fair. YES takers paid
  12.9¢ on average for outcomes that hit 12.5% of the time. No leg-count bucket is
  significant (2 legs +0.02¢; 3–4 +0.53¢; 5–7 −0.90¢; 8+ +0.54¢).
- **Why the press margin doesn't show:** the "26.5% combo margin" is a quote-level
  figure, and maker competition in the request-for-quote auction appears to compete
  it away on the trades that happen.

**Not pursued further** unless a forward record over more weeks keeps the second
half's sign.
