No price-only strategy (taker or maker, followed or inverted) in any Kalshi
category or series survives out-of-sample once real costs and real fills are applied.

## Data
- 20 sampled days, offsets 2…116 days before 2026-10-01 (June 7 – Sep 29).
  Discovery = 10 older days, confirmation = 10 newer days.
- 2,007 series, 27,464 sampled events, 152,703 markets, 208,728 entries meeting the
  quote rule (two-sided, spread ≤ 10¢, mid in [0.03, 0.97]).
- Entry times are fractions of the scheduled lifetime (open → expected
  expiration), never of the actual close, so early-resolving markets do not leak
  their outcome.

## Maker grid and the fill check

roughly "taker + the full spread". Thirteen category cells and two series cells
passed both halves on that basis. The trade tape then shows which bids actually
fill. "Through" means a later trade printed strictly through our price, so the
fill is certain regardless of queue position. "Touch" also counts trades at our
price, which is optimistic.

All of the apparent maker edge sits in the bids that never fill. A resting bid gets
hit when informed flow knows the price should be lower. This is the same adverse
selection measured on tennis and MLB in `docs/MARKOUT_2026-09-23.md`, now shown
across every category.

## Calibration (descriptive, not a test)
Settle rate minus quoted mid, by price bucket, pooled over entry times:

- **Sports** (38,080 event-buckets): every bucket within ±0.4¢. Effectively perfectly
  calibrated.
- **Weather / Commodities / Crypto / Economics**: a favourite–longshot pattern.
  Favourites (≥ 85¢) settle 1.5–4¢ above mid; longshots (< 15¢) settle 1.3–4.5¢
  below, except Crypto, where they are on the mark.
  The quoted spread is 3–5¢ and the fee is up to 1.75¢, so a taker's cost is about
  2–4¢. The bias is real but smaller than the toll, and resting orders that would
  harvest it only get filled when it is absent.

## Other checks run in this round
- **Basket arbitrage on 4,983 open mutually exclusive events** (live, 2026-10-01).
  Buying NO on every leg, which pays at least k − 1 under mutual exclusivity
  alone, never had a positive edge with size. Buying YES on every leg looked
  positive only on non-exhaustive candidate lists (unlisted outcomes pay nothing).
  The closest thing to an exhaustive pair, the state AG races, leaves ~0.6¢ that
  prices the third-party / no-swearing-in tail until Jan 2027: about 2%/yr, not
  arbitrage.

## Integrity notes
- Two earlier runs used a broken split (discovery held only 2–4 days, because
  `/markets` stops listing settlements older than ~70 days and the batch candle
  endpoint returns nothing past the historical cutoff). Both runs are void. They
  did, however, show me confirmation-half numbers for some cells. The only cells
  that passed confirmation in the corrected run are maker cells, and all of those
  are killed by the fill check, which is independent of that exposure.
- Remaining biases: final volume > 0 filter; daily candles for markets lasting more
  than 7 days (quotes up to a day stale, but never from the future); 1-contract
  size with no queue model (the "through" fill rule is the conservative bound).

## What is left
Every hypothesis based on public prices alone (19 across this and earlier
rounds) is negative. A durable edge would have to come from information the book
has not priced yet: a source that moves before Kalshi quotes do (step 2 of the
plan, the MLB news-timing recorder), measured with the same pre-registration and
forward paper testing.
