# Rebuild the Engine Around Fills, Not Forecasts

Our engine has no repeatable edge today. Neither does any public model or repo we could find against a liquid US-legal sports price. Our own pre-registered tests and the literature agree on where edge sits on Kalshi and Polymarket US in late 2026: with **fast, reference-anchored liquidity providers who cancel before they are picked off**, and with in-play traders who react within about five seconds. It does not sit with forecasters who cross spreads. Our tests have now covered more than twenty hypotheses on real venue data: Elo/MLB/tennis models, favourite–longshot fades, slow maker quoting, liquidity-incentive capture, weather nowcasts, crypto Up/Down systems, copying sharp wallets, cross-venue lag, and combo making. Every one failed out of sample once real fees and real fills were applied. Kalshi sports prices are calibrated to within **±0.4¢** in every price bucket ([SURVEY_2026-10-01](../docs/SURVEY_2026-10-01.md)). The literature's positive maker returns (Bürgi/Deng/Whelan's **+2.6% after fees** for Kalshi makers buying at 50¢ or above; Becker's **+1.12% per trade** across 72.1M trades) are averages over every resting order, and professional makers who requote in milliseconds dominate them. Our fill-level tests show a slow, small maker gets filled mainly when it is wrong. The restructure is therefore a change of business model, from "predict and take" to "quote passively on Polymarket US and Kalshi, anchored to the leading venue and a sharp sportsbook reference, and cancel faster than the information arrives". The first real money should be a small, hard-capped **measurement pilot that buys fill and markout data**, which no simulation can produce. It should not be a profit-seeking deployment. With unlimited capital the answer is a registered market-making operation, and capital alone buys none of its edge: that comes from latency, official data, venue program terms and order flow.

## Our own tests show the prices are already right

The repo's own experiments are the strongest evidence available. They are pre-registered, split into discovery and confirmation, and run on the venues we would actually trade. Taken together they show these markets are efficient to within about one tick. Any strategy that must cross a spread and pay a taker fee faces a toll of 2–3% that the available mispricings do not cover ([STRATEGY_LAB_2026-09-29](../docs/STRATEGY_LAB_2026-09-29.md)).

The predictive core of the current engine has no information the price lacks. On 873 Kalshi MLB games, the regression of the market's error on the model's disagreement has **beta +0.065 (t 0.20)**. A model with real edge would show a beta near 1. Blending the model into the price makes the forecast worse at every weight ([EDGE_VERDICT Result 2](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). On a fresh month of Polymarket MLB games the slope is negative at every lead. The bootstrapped tennis model scores worse than the base rate and loses about **18% per bet** (t ≈ −2.2). The NWS weather arm is the repo's best signal: it beats climatology on **9 of 10 complete forward dates**, but it still loses to the market on Brier (0.1408 vs 0.0802). It carries nothing the price lacks either (beta 0.036, t 0.43) ([SIGNALS_2026-09-17](../docs/SIGNALS_2026-09-17.md); [EDGE_VERDICT Result 7](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). Beating climatology on ten dates is a small forward sample, and it answers a different question from beating the market.

The structural ideas fail for a more instructive reason. In the exchange-wide Kalshi survey, 13 category cells and 2 series cells passed both halves under a "every resting bid fills" assumption. Every one of them turned negative once fills were taken from the real trade tape. Weather YES favourites went from **+5.5¢ to −6.8¢** per contract, and KXATPSETWINNER from **+3.0¢ to −19.3¢**. Meanwhile the bids that never filled would have earned +8¢ to +48¢ ([SURVEY_2026-10-01](../docs/SURVEY_2026-10-01.md)). That is adverse selection in its purest form: a resting order gets hit when informed flow knows it is stale. The same pattern appears in the markout study of 34,454 Kalshi tennis maker fills. Flow is **84/16 one-sided**, the median markout is zero, and the mean worsens with holding time. In pre-game MLB, flow is 92/8 ([MARKOUT_2026-09-23](../docs/MARKOUT_2026-09-23.md)).

Two results are positive, and they point the same way. First, profitable Polymarket sports wallets really are skilled. August's 52 qualifying "sharps" (about 21 expected by chance) beat the market in September at their own prices by **+2.75¢ per share, t = 3.2**, while random wallets did not. Yet 85% of their first buys are in-play, and copying them goes from +1.7¢ at 1–5 s to negative beyond 15 s ([SHARPS_2026-10-05](../docs/SHARPS_2026-10-05.md)). Second, in active pre-match tennis sessions, Polymarket's book follows Kalshi's. The gap averages only 0.6 points, so hitting the lagging ask marks out at −1.9 to −2.2 points. The only version that could pay is a resting maker order on the lagging venue ([EDGE_VERDICT Result 10](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). This is a small, session-dependent sample measured on Polymarket's international book, not on Polymarket US. Both results say the same thing: **the edge is speed and information timing, and it can be harvested passively only by a maker who knows the reference price has moved before its quote is hit.**

## The literature's maker profits are averages that a slow maker cannot capture

The literature looks like it contradicts our tests, and the apparent contradiction is the most important thing to understand before spending money. Bürgi, Deng and Whelan, on Kalshi transaction data from 2021 to about April 2025, find makers lose **−9.64%** on average against takers' **−31.46%**. Makers buying contracts at 50¢ or above earn **+2.6% after commission**, with a 33% return standard deviation, and the bias is "diminishing over time" ([GWU WP 2026-001](https://www2.gwu.edu/~forcpgm/2026-001.pdf)). Becker's 72.1M-trade Kalshi dataset (June 2021 to November 2025) finds makers **+1.12%** and takers −1.12% per trade, with a 2.23-point gap in sports. He attributes maker gains to "providing liquidity to biased taker flow, not from better forecasting" ([Becker](https://jbecker.dev/research/prediction-market-microstructure)). Bartlett and O'Hara find Kalshi makers earn about **2× per contract** in single-name markets, because traders overbet YES on markets that mostly settle NO ([Stanford Law](https://law.stanford.edu/publications/adverse-selection-in-prediction-markets-evidence-from-kalshi/)).

Five mechanisms reconcile those averages with our fill-level losses, and each one is testable.

The first is **composition**. A paper's "maker return" pools every resting order that executed, including those of professional firms that requote in milliseconds and cancel the instant a reference moves. Their stale quotes rarely execute, so their filled orders are a favourable sample. Our 1-minute quotes are the opposite sample: they stay in place while the information arrives. The CRYPTO15 test shows this directly. A 1-minute two-sided maker loses about $31 per market, while the profitable wallets doing the same activity requote in milliseconds ([CRYPTO15_2026-10-05](../docs/CRYPTO15_2026-10-05.md)).

The second is **conditioning on fills**. Our survey shows that all of the apparent maker edge sits in bids that never fill. An "average maker" statistic computed over executed trades cannot separate the fast maker who escaped from the slow maker who was hit.

The third is **queue position**. Liquid Kalshi and Polymarket sports books are one tick wide, so a new small maker joins the back of the touch queue. It is filled mainly when the price is moving through it ([EDGE_VERDICT Result 4](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)).

The fourth is **which flow pays**. The averages are driven by the favourite–longshot bias: contracts under 10¢ lose over 60% of their stake. Our survey finds sports essentially free of that bias, with every bucket within ±0.4¢. On Polymarket, sports is "the clearest counterexample" to longshot overpricing ([arXiv 2609.12878](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12878v1)). Retail longshot money mostly flows in weather, crypto, economics and novelty markets, and there our fill check still found the harvestable bias smaller than the adverse selection on filled orders.

The fifth is **time**. Becker reports that takers were ahead through 2023 and that the gap flipped to makers only after the 2024 election ([Becker](https://jbecker.dev/research/prediction-market-microstructure)). Both datasets end before 2026, when Jump, Susquehanna, Flutter, Crypto.com and DraftKings affiliates moved into making ([Finance Magnates/FT](https://www.financemagnates.com/fintech/wall-street-quants-move-into-prediction-markets-to-hunt-for-arbitrage-not-to-bet/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/389086/jump-trading-to-serve-as-polymarket-and-kalshi-market-maker-in-exchange-for-stake-bloomberg)). Kalshi is ending its Volume Incentive Program early, and DKeX combo makers reportedly "no longer need rebates", which suggests margins are compressing toward what fast incumbents can earn ([PMP Weekly](https://predictionmarketpulse.substack.com/p/pmp-weekly-october-5-2026)).

The reconciliation produces a falsifiable prediction that our next experiment should test. **Markouts on filled maker orders should improve monotonically as cancel latency falls and as quotes are gated on a stable reference price.** If a reference-anchored maker with sub-second cancels still shows a non-positive median markout, the literature's averages are unreachable for us at any speed we can build, and the maker thesis dies. The same logic applies to the Moshrefi finding that Kalshi sports moneylines are miscalibrated pre-game and in the final minutes ([arXiv 2607.14430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14430)), and to Bartlett and O'Hara's YES-overbetting. Our endgame test was negative on 639 independent games ([STRATEGY_LAB](../docs/STRATEGY_LAB_2026-09-29.md)). Our Mentions YES-overbet effect passed in September and then failed on disjoint June–August data on "through" fills ([LIP_HIST](../docs/LIP_HIST_2026-10-03.md)). Those patterns are real in the aggregate, but by the time a small participant can rest an order against them, faster makers have absorbed them.

## Restructure into a reference-anchored, cancel-first liquidity engine

The engine should stop treating its own forecast as the fair price. Every documented profitable approach combines market prices with something decorrelated. Benter's model lost money until he blended it with public odds ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/bill-benter/)). Hubáček's NBA model profited by being trained to *differ* from bookmaker odds while being less accurate ([IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/intfor/v35y2019i2p783-796.html)). Kaunitz et al. beat bookmakers by betting outliers against a multi-book consensus, earning **8.5% over five months of real money** before being stopped ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/s/609168/the-secret-betting-strategy-that-beats-online-bookmakers/); [Cosmos](https://cosmosmagazine.com/mathematics/researchers-work-out-how-to-break-the-bookmakers)). AIA Forecaster underperforms the market alone on liquid questions, but an AIA-plus-market ensemble beats it ([arXiv 2511.07678](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07678v1)). No open-source sports model publishes an audited out-of-sample record against closing lines. The only closing-line study found in the repo survey reports a negative result ([AnishKhetani/football-model](https://github.com/AnishKhetani/football-model)).

The reference layer should be built from the leading venue's book: Kalshi leads Polymarket in-play by about 3 s and pre-match in active sessions ([LAG_2026-10-05](../docs/LAG_2026-10-05.md)). A de-vigged sharp sportsbook feed should sit alongside it. Pinnacle closed its public API in July 2025, but Pinnacle-only push feeds cost **$99–229/month** ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/ryankr/pinnacle-killed-its-public-api-heres-how-to-get-pinnacle-odds-in-2026-with-code-39pe)). The Elo, Markov and pitcher models should feed the reference only as a residual whose weight is fit on past data and set to zero unless its out-of-sample beta is positive. On the current evidence that weight is zero.

The quoting layer is the new core. It posts only post-only orders on Polymarket US (`participateDontInitiate`), where makers pay nothing and earn a rebate at Θ = 0.0125 against a taker Θ of 0.0695 ([Polymarket US fees](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)). On Kalshi, where about 160 game series charge makers a quarter of the taker fee ([EDGE_VERDICT Result 11](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)), it uses `post_only`. It quotes only when the reference has been stable over a lookback window, skews toward the reference, and cancels first whenever the reference moves or a scheduled information window opens.

`warproxxx/poly-maker` (MIT) has the most portable design to borrow. It runs a regime machine (quiet, trending, event-pull, reduce-only, halted), joint YES/NO inventory skew, spread widening driven by toxicity, and a heartbeat dead-man switch. The `kachence/polymm` post-mortem names cancel speed and partial-fill residuals as where its losses came from ([poly-maker](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker); [polymm](https://github.com/kachence/polymm)).

Public scheduled information should be used **defensively first**. The quoting layer should pull quotes around NBA injury-report deadlines ([NBA](https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2025-26/)), MLB lineup postings and tennis order-of-play releases, because those windows are when resting orders get picked off. Offensive use (taking after a public release) remains a research question for the MLB news-timing recorder already in the plan. Kalshi enforces against trading on nonpublic information, with about 200 investigations in a year ([A&O Shearman](https://www.lit-wc.aoshearman.com/siteFiles/54154/Two%20Insider%20Cases%20We%E2%80%99ve%20Recently%20Closed%20-%20Kalshi%20Article%20(Feb.%2025%202026).pdf)), so the engine should use only timestamped public sources.

Three supporting changes are needed. A markout and fill-measurement layer must run on every strategy, marking each fill at +5 s, +60 s, +5 min and +30 min, which extends `backtest/markout.py` to live fills. The repo needs a **new Polymarket US client**: the current `polymarket.py` targets the geoblocked international CLOB. The official `polymarket-us` Python SDK uses Ed25519 keys generated at polymarket.us/developer and supports post-only, IOC/FOK, cancel-all and WebSockets ([polymarket-us-python](https://github.com/Polymarket/polymarket-us-python); [auth docs](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/authentication.md)). Finally, risk limits must be aggregated at the account level across `sportsbot`, `kalshi-engine` and `polymarket-edge`. Enforcement actions under SEC Rule 15c3-5 single out limits that are not aggregated across entry points ([FINRA CGMI action](https://WWW.FINRA.ORG/sites/default/files/CGMI_action_documents_072617.pdf)).

The large language model (LLM) judge already running in shadow (`sportsbot-judge.service`) should stay forward-only and blended. On 104 World Cup matches none of four frontier models beat the bookmaker ([arXiv 2607.17765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17765)), and backtests of LLMs are contaminated by leaked outcomes ([arXiv 2607.14051](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14051)).

Several things should be dropped or frozen, because evidence already rules them out. Standalone taker betting on model edges goes. So do favourite–longshot fades in sports, Kalshi LIP capture, weather nowcasts and forecast-only weather bets (whose settlement source is now The Weather Company, as Kalshi's KXHIGHNY-26OCT01 rules text states), crypto Up/Down systems, wallet copying, and cross-venue taker arbitrage. The `arb-scanner` found only wrong-question pairs and multi-year lockups, and its fee constants are out of date ([EDGE_VERDICT Result 11](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). No Polymarket order should ever route through the international CLOB. On open source, no repo in the survey reached verified live profitability. The space also carries documented malware, including a hijacked GitHub org whose Polymarket bot stole `.env` keys through typosquatted npm dependencies ([StepSecurity](https://www.stepsecurity.io/blog/malicious-polymarket-bot-hides-in-hijacked-dev-protocol-github-org-and-steals-wallet-keys)), and an impersonating `dev-polymarket/clob-client-v2` with more stars than the official repo ([GitHub](https://github.com/dev-polymarket/clob-client-v2)). Use only official SDKs, read third-party code before vendoring it, and install with `--ignore-scripts`.

Two candidates are worth keeping alive as forward records only. Kalshi combos had a positive confirmation half (+1.11¢, day-t 2.04) after a negative discovery half. Combo volume is enormous (about $2B on record days, with Flutter about 15% of it) ([COMBO_2026-10-05](../docs/COMBO_2026-10-05.md); [PMP Weekly](https://predictionmarketpulse.substack.com/p/pmp-weekly-october-5-2026)). The other is the ITF tennis 0.70–0.80 cell flagged for an end-of-October re-run. Neither earns capital until a pre-registered forward test passes.

## A small live pilot should buy fill data, not profit

The one question simulation cannot answer is how a real resting order fills, and at what markout, given queue position and cancel speed ([EDGE_VERDICT](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). Kalshi's demo exchange is unrepresentative: its docs warn prices "may not be reflective of those in real markets" ([Kalshi demo docs](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env)). Polymarket US retail has no public sandbox. The first live stage should therefore be treated as a budgeted measurement exercise, a deliberate data purchase with a known maximum cost.

The current go-live gate (200 settled paper bets plus positive CLV) was designed for taker strategies and cannot be met by a maker strategy in paper mode. Whether a pre-registered, hard-capped measurement pilot satisfies that gate is a decision for the account owner. The pilot must not weaken anything. It keeps `mode: live` plus `SPORTSBOT_LIVE=1` (or `live: true` plus `--live` for the engine suite), the `pre-live-gate` checklist, fail-closed risk checks, and loss responses that only reduce risk.

The primary hypothesis, H-A, comes from EDGE_VERDICT Result 10. It is a Polymarket US post-only maker on tennis and MLB moneylines in active pre-match sessions, skewed toward the Kalshi mid and cancelled on any Kalshi move or scheduled news window. The secondary hypothesis, H-B, runs the same logic on Kalshi to compare queue behaviour at a quarter-fee maker cost.

The primary metric is the mean net markout per filled contract at 60 s and at 5 minutes, including the rebate or fee as charged. The comparison baseline is the "never-filled" counterfactual, so adverse selection is measured directly. Secondary metrics are fill rate, cancel-to-acknowledgement latency, queue position at fill, CLV against the last two-sided pre-start mid, and realized P&L. Because P&L on near-50¢ binaries has a standard deviation of about $0.50 per contract, detecting a 3¢ edge from P&L alone takes about 1,700 trades. By contrast, the repo measured pre-game CLV with an SD of 0.0222, which needs only about **79 observations to detect half a point** ([EDGE_VERDICT](../docs/EDGE_VERDICT_2026-09-23.md)). Markout and CLV are therefore the decision metrics, and P&L is a sanity check.

Whelan's August 2026 NBA study shows why the bar must be "net of costs" rather than "positive". His ninth CLV decile averaged 5% CLV but only 0.2% profit ([Karl Whelan](https://www.karlwhelan.com/?p=2595)).

Several operational details will bite a first live run. Kalshi pauses trading every Thursday from 3:00 to 5:00 AM ET. Taxes are unsettled. Get a trader-tax CPA's view before scaling volume. Finally, check that your own state is not one where sports contracts are being enforced against. After the Ninth and Sixth Circuit rulings, Nevada, Ohio, Tennessee and tribal lands are live risks ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/ninth-circuit-upholds-state-and-tribal-authority-over-sports-related); [SBC Americas](https://sbcamericas.com/2026/10/05/ohio-prediction-markets-crackdown-win)).

## Unlimited capital buys infrastructure and access, not foresight

With no capital constraint, the rational structure is a market-making firm, not a bigger betting bankroll. The firm would be an LLC trading proprietary capital, onboarded directly with each DCM. It would apply to Kalshi's market-maker program, which offers reduced fees and adjusted position limits in exchange for 98% two-sided availability in each hour across listed products ([Kalshi Help Center](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823819-how-to-become-a-market-maker-on-kalshi)). It would apply to Polymarket US's Market Maker and Liquidity Provider programs through institutional@qcex.com ([Polymarket US docs](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/market-maker); [CFTC filing](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/orgrules/26/03/rules03032640252.pdf)). It would use Polymarket US's institutional exchange-trader API, which offers a preprod environment and FIX over AWS PrivateLink ([Polymarket US environments](https://docs.polymarket.us/trader-guide/environments.md)), and Kalshi FIX. Kalshi's PrivateLink and its 100–400 writes/s tiers are gated on 3.75–7.5% of monthly exchange volume ([Kalshi rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits); [Kalshi FIX](https://docs.kalshi.com/fix-margin/connectivity)), so scale itself buys the infrastructure.

The firm would license official low-latency data. Genius claims 4–6 s behind the field on its primary NFL stream, about 30–40 s ahead of TV ([Vixio](https://www.vixio.com/insights/gc-genius-sports-launches-live-betting-product-leagues-search-gaming-integrations)). It would also buy enterprise sharp-odds push feeds and build an RFQ engine for combos, the one flow where takers buy YES 82% of the time at volume. It would extend to the newer venues during their subsidy phases: Rothera, which passed 5B contracts within four months; Crypto.com/OG's incentive program; and DKeX ([PMP Weekly](https://predictionmarketpulse.substack.com/p/pmp-weekly-october-5-2026)). An internal digest reports that Crypto.com exempts market makers from a 3-s retail live delay ([Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2025/12/11/116729-cryptocom-implements-a-threesecond-delay-for-retail-users-in-its-sports-eventbetting-market)). That is the one structural speed privilege found, and it needs rulebook confirmation. Sportsbook and insurer hedging flow, such as Game Point Capital's planned $30M a year through Kalshi, is price-insensitive flow an MM can quote into ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/389841/kalshi-inks-sports-hedging-deal-with-game-point-on-the-heels-of-over-1-billion-in-super-bowl-trading)).

There is a real profit pool. Flutter expects about **$50M of market-making EBITDA in 2026** ([DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/flutter-moves-fanduel-predicts-sports-contracts-crypto-com/)). Industry volume exceeds **$290B over the trailing 52 weeks, about 85% of it sports** ([PMP Weekly](https://predictionmarketpulse.substack.com/p/pmp-weekly-october-5-2026)). Incumbents also hold equity in the venues: Jump's equity-for-liquidity deals, and Susquehanna's co-ownership of Rothera ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/options/articles/rothera-robinhood-prediction-market-empire-125229625.html)). They earn exchange economics on top of spread, so a pure-prop entrant should target props, combos and new venues rather than headline NFL and NBA moneylines.

| What is missing today | Why it matters | How to close it |
|---|---|---|
| Fill and markout data on Polymarket US and Kalshi | The literature's maker edge is unverifiable for us without it | The live pilot above; continuous L2 capture (`maker/` scripts) |
| A queue-aware L2 simulator | Candle and trade backtests cannot see queue position | Record full books plus our own fills; calibrate fill models to live data |
| Sub-second reference-to-cancel latency | The sharp edge decays within ~5 s; slow quotes are the losing side | AWS us-east-2 hosting, WebSockets, FIX at higher tiers |

| Official data and sharp push feeds | In-play and news edges need a feed ahead of TV and scrapers | Pinnacle push feed now; enterprise feeds only once quoting pays |
| MM agreements, rebates, position limits | Fee and limit terms decide whether a thin edge clears | Apply once a pilot shows positive markouts |
| Legal and tax structure | Circuit split, SCOTUS cert No. 26-299, NY AG suit against Polymarket; gambling-loss limit | Derivatives counsel; trader-tax CPA, possibly a §475 election |
| Correlation-aware portfolio risk | Same-game legs and same-day weather bands are one bet | Event-level caps already partly present; extend across engines |

The legal risk is binary and near-term. New Jersey's certiorari petition is docketed as No. 26-299, with Kalshi's response due November 9 ([SiaPredict](https://siapredict.com/blog/kalshi-supreme-court-flaherty-docket-26-299)). A Supreme Court grant would put the whole sports-contract business in question. The CFTC has sent a proposed rule defining event contracts as swaps to the White House ([PMP Weekly](https://predictionmarketpulse.substack.com/p/pmp-weekly-october-5-2026)).

Several things remain uncertain. Polymarket US's own book dynamics, settlement timing and 1099 practice are unmeasured. The Polymarket-follows-Kalshi pattern rests on four active half-hours on a different book. Whether Kalshi offers a session-level cancel-on-disconnect is unconfirmed. Program terms (stipends, spread obligations, rate-limit grants) are redacted or unpublished.

## Conclusion

The research changes what the engine is for. It is not a better forecaster, because the price already contains everything our models and public signals know. Its job is to measure and minimise adverse selection, and the experiment that decides its future is cheap: a few hundred dollars of resting orders, instrumented for markouts and cancel latency, on the one venue pair where we have a measured information lead. If faster, reference-gated quotes produce positive net markouts, the path to scale is concrete: hosting, feeds, MM programs, combos. If they do not, the honest conclusion is that this edge belongs to firms with exchange equity and official data, and the best use of the stack is as a measurement and research instrument rather than a trading business.

One forward-looking point. Every structural mispricing in the literature (longshot bias, single-name YES overbetting, last-minute miscalibration, parlay markups) was measured on data that ends before the 2026 influx of institutional market makers, and in our 2026 data each one is either gone in sports or absorbed before a slow order can reach it. The pre-registered, fill-level discipline the repo has built is rarer than any model in the public record. It should be kept for every future idea, including the ones that look most like free money.
